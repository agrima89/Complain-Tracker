HOW IT WORKS

Transparent 4-Stage Redressal Pipeline

From reporting to student confirmation, every grievance is tracked in real time.

1️⃣

Submit Ticket

Choose location, category, describe the issue, and attach photo evidence.

2️⃣

Admin Dispatch

Relevant department HOD assigns technicians and updates lifecycle status to In Progress.

3️⃣

Resolution Submitted

Maintenance fixes the problem and marks resolution with work remarks.

4️⃣

Student Confirmation

Student confirms fix or regenerates the ticket if not satisfied.
