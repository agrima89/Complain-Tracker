Our promise

We are CampusCare, a student-built helpdesk. We turn everyday complaints into real fixes, with every step on record.

Every complaint on campus is routed to the right department, merged with reports of the same problem and tracked in the open. Students see each update, staff get a clear queue and the whole campus gets a little better every week. No lost emails, no chasing anyone at the office counter.

Campus-grade workflows for every student. Built for clarity. Powered by care.
